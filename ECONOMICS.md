at a 20-30% discount to retail, and takes a margin on that discount:

- Average deal size: $2,000-5,000 in credits purchased upfront
- Credex margin assumption: 15-25% on the discount spread

Optimistic estimate: **$2,000 LTV** if the customer repurchases and
refers others.

and one-time buyers.

## The conversion funnel

| Stage | Conversion | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Landing page visit | 100% | Baseline |
| Audit form started | 40% | Strong hook, low friction |
| Audit completed | 70% of starters | Form is fast and lightweight |
| Email captured | 25% of completers | Value shown before asking |
| Opens consultation link | 15% of email captures | Only shown for high-savings audits |
| Books consultation | 40% of link openers | Users are already highly qualified |
| Purchases credits | 50% of consultations | Warm inbound leads |

Working through the funnel:

- 1,000 visitors
- 400 start the audit
- 280 complete it
- 70 capture email
- ~15 see the high-savings Credex CTA
- 6 click the consultation link
- 2–3 book a call
- 1–2 purchase credits

**Estimated conversion rate:** landing page → purchase = ~0.1–0.2%

At a projected $750 LTV per customer:

- 1,000 visitors → 1–2 customers → ~$750–1,500 revenue
- Estimated revenue per visitor: ~$0.75–1.50

## CAC at each channel

**Reddit posts (organic):**
- Cost: 2-3 hours of writing time
- Expected visitors per post: 200-500 if it resonates
- CAC: effectively $0 cash, ~$30-50 in time value
- Verdict: best channel for early traction

**Twitter/X threads:**
- Cost: 1-2 hours writing, zero cash
- Expected visitors: 100-300 from a genuine thread
- CAC: $0 cash
- Verdict: compounds over time as threads get reshared

**Product Hunt launch:**
- Cost: 3-4 hours preparation
- Expected visitors: 500-2,000 on launch day
- CAC: $0 cash, high time investment
- Verdict: one-time spike, good for social proof

**Paid search (future):**
- Keywords: "ai tool pricing", "cursor vs copilot cost", "chatgpt team pricing"
- Estimated CPC: $2-5 for these terms
- At 0.15% conversion rate: CAC = $1,300-3,300
- Verdict: not viable until LTV is better understood

**Credex vendor relationships (unfair channel):**
- Cost: $0 — relationship already exists
- Mechanism: pricing change announcements drive search traffic
- CAC: near zero
- Verdict: highest priority after product-market fit confirmed

## What needs to be true for $1M ARR in 18 months

$1M ARR = ~$83,000/month in revenue to Credex.

At $750 LTV per customer and assuming customers transact twice per year:
- Annual revenue per customer: $750
- Customers needed: 1,333

To acquire 1,333 customers in 18 months = ~74 new customers per month.

Working back through the funnel:
- 74 purchases/month
- Requires ~148 consultations/month (50% close rate)
- Requires ~370 consultation link clicks/month (40% book rate)
- Requires ~2,467 email captures/month (15% see high-savings CTA)
- Requires ~9,867 audit completions/month
- Requires ~35,000 visitors/month

**Is 35,000 visitors/month achievable in 18 months?**

Realistically, yes — but it requires:

1. The Reddit and Twitter strategy working consistently, not just once.
   Assume 2-3 viral posts per month averaging 3,000 visitors each = 6,000-9,000/month from organic social.

2. SEO kicking in by month 6. Pages targeting "cursor pricing 2026",
   "chatgpt team vs enterprise", "github copilot cost" can realistically
   hit 500-1,000 visitors/month each by month 9-12.

3. The Credex vendor channel delivering 5,000-10,000 visitors on pricing
   announcement days, 3-4 times per year.

4. Word of mouth from satisfied users. At 10,000 audits/month, if 2%
   share their result publicly, that is 200 organic shares per month.

**What has to go right:**
- Product Hunt launch generates meaningful traffic and backlinks
- At least one pricing change announcement goes semi-viral
- Email open rates stay above 30% (report emails are high-value, this is achievable)
- Credex sales team closes at 50%+ once leads arrive qualified

**What kills it:**
- AI tool pricing stabilizes and nobody is searching for comparisons
- A major player (Stripe, Ramp) builds this into their existing spend dashboard
- Credex margin on credits compresses as the market matures

## Honest summary

The rough unit economics appear viable if the funnel assumptions are directionally correct. The biggest uncertainty is not the math — it is whether 35,000 monthly visitors
is achievable through organic channels alone. Paid acquisition does
not work at current LTV estimates. The business lives or dies on the
Credex unfair channel and consistent organic distribution.

The tool needs to be genuinely useful — not just a lead magnet — or
the audit completion rate drops and the whole funnel collapses.
