## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
Yes. No signup, no credit card, no freemium gate. The audit runs
instantly and results are shown immediately. Email is only asked for
after you've seen your results.

**Q: Do you store my spend data?**
Audit calculations happen client-side for speed and transparency.
Data is only persisted when you choose to save a shareable report or
capture the report via email. Public report pages intentionally strip
private information like email addresses and company names.

**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**
We manually verify pricing from each vendor's official pricing page
and note the verification date on every data point. AI tool pricing
changes frequently — always confirm with the vendor before acting
on a recommendation.

**Q: What tools are supported?**
Cursor, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, GitHub Copilot, OpenAI API,
Anthropic API, Windsurf, and v0. More tools added regularly.

**Q: What if I have a high-savings audit?**
For audits showing over $500/month in potential savings, we surface
Credex as an option — a service that sources discounted AI credits
from companies that overforecast. You are never required to use it.
The audit value stands on its own regardless.
